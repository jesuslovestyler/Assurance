“My name is written in heaven. I’m with Jesus.”

When fear returns in the night, come back to what was settled.

01

You may know.

“That you may know that you have eternal life.”
1 John 5:13

Assurance is God’s gift to every believer. He wrote this so that you may know you have eternal life — not guess, not feel, not earn.

02

Your name is written in heaven.

“Do not rejoice in this, that the spirits are subject to you, but rejoice that your names are written in heaven.”
Luke 10:20

Jesus separates experience from the foundation. The Spirit’s power at work is real, and it is worth rejoicing in — but it is not the ground of your salvation.

God in His kindness sometimes answers prayer with healing, and sometimes He does not. Neither outcome measures His love for you, or the certainty of your salvation.

Experiences are fruit, not foundation. Your confidence is Christ’s finished work alone — and your joy is that your name is written in heaven.

03

You are known by God.

“If anyone loves God, he is known by God.”
1 Corinthians 8:3

This is the direct answer to “I never knew you.” You love God. You are known by God.

04

Why “I never knew you” does not describe the believer.

Their plea

“Did we not prophesy in your name… and do many mighty works?” Their plea was a résumé of works.

The believer’s confidence

Not what you did, but what Christ finished. Not a record of works, but grace received by faith.

  • “Never” means never. They were never known at all; this is not a believer who was known and then lost.
  • They stood on their works. When Jesus asked for an account, they pointed to what they had done — never to what He had done for them.
  • They were workers of lawlessness. Their mighty works stood beside lives without repentance.
  • You love God and are known by God. That is the direct opposite of “I never knew you.”
05

The blasphemy of the Holy Spirit.

“For they were saying, ‘He has an unclean spirit.’”
Mark 3:30

They saw the Spirit’s clear work through Jesus and called it demonic. This was a settled, willful verdict — not a doubt, not a slip, and not an intrusive thought.

Those who commit it do not fear they have committed it. A heart running to Jesus is the opposite of its character. The Spirit in you testifies for Christ, not against Him.

06

What is settled for every believer.

Chosen with full knowledge

God saw every sin beforehand, and chose you anyway.

The finished work

Christ’s finished work covers all sin.

Faith is received

Even faith itself is God’s gift — received, not earned.

“It’s all grace.”

“My name is written in heaven. I’m with Jesus.”
