# Assurance

A quiet, mobile-friendly anchor page for nights when assurance wavers — the verses and settled answers about whose we are in Christ, gathered in one kept place.

Includes:

- **1 John 5:13** — "that you may know that you have eternal life" — know, not guess, not feel, not earn
- **Luke 10:20** — "rejoice that your names are written in heaven" — the miracle is fruit, not foundation
- **1 Corinthians 8:3** — "if anyone loves God, he is known by God" — the direct answer to "I never knew you"
- **Matthew 7:23** — why it doesn't apply: "never" means never had the relationship; their plea was a résumé of works; confidence rests on Christ's finished work, not our own
- **The blasphemy of the Holy Spirit** (Mark 3:28–30) — calling the Spirit's clear work demonic: a settled, willful verdict, not a doubt or an intrusive thought

Scripture quotations are from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation.
